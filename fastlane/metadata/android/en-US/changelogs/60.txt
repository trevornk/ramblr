New optional bubble shortcut and a more compact long-press menu.

- Bubble shortcut: pick an app in Settings > Starting dictation > Floating icon. The menu gets an "Open <app>" row. Off by default; nothing is passed to the app.
- Long-press menu: styles collapse into one row.
- OpenAI retires whisper-1 and gpt-4o-transcribe on Feb 26, 2027. If you chose one directly, Ramblr moves you to gpt-transcribe. Custom endpoints are untouched.
- WhatsApp search: text lands at the right cursor.
