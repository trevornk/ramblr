New optional bubble shortcut: pick an app in Settings > Starting dictation > Floating icon > Bubble shortcut and the icon's long-press menu gets a big "Open <app>" row at the top.

- Off by default; nothing changes until you choose an app.
- Launches the app plain: no dictation text or context is passed to it.
- If the app is uninstalled, the setting reverts to "Not set".
- The long-press menu is more compact: styles are grouped into one row that expands when tapped.
