New optional bubble shortcut: pick any app in Settings > Behavior and the icon's long-press menu gets an "Open <app>" row that launches it.

- Off by default; nothing changes until you choose an app.
- Launches the app plain: no dictation text or context is passed to it, and it does not start a dictation.
- If the chosen app is uninstalled, the setting reverts to "Not set" and the row disappears.
