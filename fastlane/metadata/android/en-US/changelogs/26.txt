Fixes for the F-Droid review.

- Long dictations no longer fail silently. Speech is now transcribed in
  segments instead of all at once, which keeps memory flat regardless of how
  long you talk. Previously a several-minute recording could exhaust memory and
  be lost with no transcription and no error.
- Every downloadable model now states its license before you download it, and
  the optional cleanup model whose license restricts commercial use asks for
  explicit consent first. Transcription models are all freely licensed.
- The app description now lists every network connection the app can make and
  what triggers each one.
