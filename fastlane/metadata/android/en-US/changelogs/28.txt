Privacy fix: no cloud connection when you have not configured one.

- Ramblr opened a connection to OpenAI's servers ahead of each dictation even
  when you had no API key saved, cleanup switched off, and transcription set to
  on-device only. Nothing was ever sent over that connection, but opening it
  revealed your IP address and the fact that you use Ramblr to a company you had
  chosen not to involve. Ramblr now prepares a connection only to a service it
  could actually reach on your behalf.
