Android 16 support, faster cleanup, and fixes from the F-Droid review.

- Now targets Android 16 (API 36), so the app keeps working on the newest
  release and stays installable from app stores. Settings and the system bars
  render correctly under Android's forced edge-to-edge layout.
- Choosing a cleanup model no longer selects it before you agree to its
  license. Cancelling the license prompt now leaves your setup untouched, and
  the model's license is stated in the picker.
- The recommended on-device cleanup model now downloads normally instead of
  needing a manual install, and is offered under a free Apache-2.0 license.
- Cleanup gives up faster when a provider stalls: the hard cap dropped from 15
  to 8 seconds, and cloud cleanup can no longer overrun the remaining budget,
  so a slow provider delays your text far less.
- Dictating into a field that already holds a draft now inserts a space instead
  of running the words together, and apps that draw their own "Message"
  placeholder (WhatsApp) no longer get that placeholder treated as real text.
- Your custom vocabulary now actually reaches the on-device cleanup model.
- The floating bubble's peek edge is easier to hit and no longer gets stranded
  off-screen after rotating the device.
- Recovering the app when its icon is hidden now works even if you swipe the
  notification away, and the Quick Settings tile fixes two crashes.
- Local transcription now provisions its own voice-detection model, and failed
  transcriptions record why they failed.
